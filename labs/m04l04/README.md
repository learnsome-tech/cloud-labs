### Write a compute decision

1. Choose a workload and describe its trigger, duration, and state.
2. Compare a machine, container, and function for that workload.
3. Record the operational burden and failure mode for your choice.

> **Hint:** Use the workload's requirements and traffic shape as the evidence.

## Check yourself

- What responsibility does a virtual machine add?
- Why can function scaling overload a dependency?
- When is a container boundary useful?
- Which cost shapes differ across the three choices?
- What should a compute decision record include?
