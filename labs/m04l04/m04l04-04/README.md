## Goal

You can compare virtual machines, containers, and functions by control, operational burden, scaling shape, and workload fit.

In the lesson: This local command prints the three cost shapes without calling a cloud service. A virtual machine is charged while capacity is allocated, whether the process is busy or idle. A container service adds the cost of its capacity and any managed control plane. A function is priced by requests and execution duration, subject to its free tier and configuration. The cheapest line depends on traffic, idle time, memory, and operational work. Use estimates and observed usage rather than assuming that serverless is always cheaper or that a busy machine is always wasteful. Cost is one input to the decision, not the decision itself.
