# Exercises — How Bills Actually Get Large

Lesson `m07l02` · [Watch](https://learnsome.tech/courses/cloud-course/watch?lesson=m07l02)

## Exercise 1: Trace one surprising charge

1. Choose one billing line and record its unit, quantity and owner.
2. Identify its lifetime, volume, price and repetition multipliers.
3. Choose one reversible change and estimate its effect.
4. Add an expiry or schedule so the charge cannot quietly return.

> **Hint**: Do not start by deleting a resource; start by explaining why the quantity exists.
